Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent at any time, before STAYTICK TRAVEL SL as data controller, by writing to dmc@staytick.com. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

FAQ

Questions travel agencies ask us.

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

What is a good DMC in Spain for travel agencies?

StayTick DMC is a tour operator and DMC based in Madrid. We plan groups, events, school trips, honeymoons and villa stays across Spain, including Madrid, Barcelona, Andalusia and the islands. Travel agencies get one contact and net prices.

Which incoming agency can organise group tours in Italy?

Italy is our specialty. We book hotels, coaches, guides, tours and private services from the lakes and Venice to Tuscany, Rome, the Amalfi Coast and Sicily. You can start from one of our four ready-made routes and adapt it.

Who can plan a multi-country tour in Europe for my clients?

We plan tours across several countries, such as Spain, Portugal, Italy, Switzerland, Austria, France and the Adriatic. You get one itinerary, one quote and one team. Trains and private transfers are included.

What is the difference between a DMC and a bed bank?

A DMC like StayTick DMC designs a full trip on request: groups, events, multi-country tours. A bed bank like Trekko, our sister brand, lets travel agencies book hotels, transfers and car rental online themselves at net prices. Use Trekko for simple bookings and StayTick DMC for anything that needs planning.

Can a DMC organise a corporate incentive or delegation in Madrid?

Yes. We organise incentives, meetings, private events and corporate or government delegations in Madrid and the rest of Spain and Europe. One team handles everything from the first airport transfer to the last dinner.

Who organises school and university trips to Spain or Italy?

We plan educational and sports trips for schools and universities, with coaches, guides, hotels and tickets in one plan. We also plan senior groups at a comfortable pace.

Can I book a private villa in Europe for a client through a DMC?

Yes. We offer villas across Europe, with or without staff, plus chalets and boutique hotels. Tell us the dates, the group size and the budget.

Do you offer chauffeur service and VIP airport meet-and-assist in Europe?

Yes. We arrange meet-and-assist at airports across Europe, private transfers by car, minivan or coach, and Mercedes cars with a professional driver by the hour or the day.

Do you organise trips to the USA or Japan?

Yes. In the USA we offer hotel rates for companies, private tours, theme-park packages and car or camper rental. In Japan we plan private itineraries and honeymoons in Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka with trusted local partners.

How can a travel agency in the US, Brazil, Mexico, the UK or the Gulf get a quote?

Send us your request through the form on this page, by email to booking@staytick.com or by phone on +34 643 19 53 84. We answer every day from 9:00 to 22:00 (CET), in English or Spanish. Prices are net for travel agencies, as a full package or by service.

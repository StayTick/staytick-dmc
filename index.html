Signature destination

Discover Italy with us

Sea, mountains, cities, national parks and UNESCO sites: in Italy every destination is a unique experience. We operate hotels, transportation, tours, packages, private and shared services across the whole country. Four tailor-made routes to start from:

8 nights · Northern Elegance

Milan, Lake Como, Lake Garda, Verona & Venice

Private airport pickup, the Duomo and Galleria, private boat tours to Bellagio and Villa Balbianello, Sirmione Castle, Bardolino wines, the Arena and a private gondola on the Grand Canal.

8 nights · The Heart of Italy

Florence, Tuscany & Rome

Uffizi skip-the-line, Art & Artisan tour, Chianti wineries and a Tuscan cooking class, then Colosseum, Vatican and Trastevere by night — Frecciarossa or private chauffeur between cities.

6 nights · Southern Charm

Naples, Amalfi Coast, Pompeii & Capri

Naples underground and street food, Positano, Amalfi and Ravello by boat, private Pompeii and Herculaneum, limoncello tastings and the Blue Grotto.

8 nights · Island Explorer & Dolomites

Sicily, Calabria, Sardinia — and South Tyrol

Palermo, Agrigento and Taormina; Tropea and Reggio Calabria; Costa Smeralda. Or head north: Lake Braies, Val di Funes, the South Tyrol Wine Route and the Falzarego Pass.
